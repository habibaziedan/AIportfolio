# BRD — Business Requirements Document

## 1.1 Project Information

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Project Name | Personal AI Portfolio Website |
| Project Owner | Habiba Ziedan |
| Project Type | Personal Digital Identity / Professional Portfolio |
| Main Field | Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning |

## 1.2 Business Objective

The objective is to build a personal website that establishes a professional digital identity for Habiba Ziedan as an AI and Machine Learning graduate. The website will provide a centralized platform where recruiters and other professional visitors can understand her background, technical skills, experience, and practical AI/ML projects.

## 1.3 Problem Statement

A traditional CV provides limited space to demonstrate practical projects and technical capabilities. A personal website can provide a more complete representation of a candidate by combining professional information with detailed project descriptions, technical skills, and links to relevant work. The website will therefore act as an extension of the CV and a central professional identity.

## 1.4 Target Audience

### Primary Audience
- AI/ML recruiters and hiring managers looking for AI interns, Machine Learning interns, junior AI engineers, or junior Machine Learning engineers.

### Secondary Audience
- AI/ML engineers
- Technical recruiters
- Potential collaborators
- Other students and professionals

## 1.6 Business Goals

1. Establish a professional online presence.
2. Clearly communicate an AI/ML career direction.
3. Showcase relevant technical skills.
4. Demonstrate practical AI/ML projects.
5. Present relevant education and experience.
6. Make professional contact information easily accessible.
7. Provide links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and CV.
8. Create a reusable digital asset for future applications.

## 1.9 Success Criteria

1. Visitors can understand the owner's AI/ML specialization quickly.
2. Relevant projects are clearly presented.
3. Professional information is accurate.
4. All important links work.
5. The website works on desktop and mobile.
6. The website is publicly deployed.
7. The GitHub repository contains meaningful development history.
8. GitHub commits can be traced to the PRD tasks.
9. Bugs discovered during development are documented and resolved.

## 1.10 Constraints

- The project should remain a simple personal portfolio.
- Content must represent the owner's real experience.
- AI-generated content must be reviewed before use.
- AI-generated code must be tested.
- Development must be tracked through GitHub.
